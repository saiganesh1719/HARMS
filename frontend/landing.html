About HARMS

The Healthcare Appointment & Resource Management System (HARMS) is a comprehensive solution designed to streamline hospital operations. Our platform facilitates easy appointment booking for patients, efficient schedule management for doctors, and robust administrative control over users, appointments, and hospital resources.

Our mission is to leverage technology to create a seamless, efficient, and patient-centric healthcare experience for everyone involved.
